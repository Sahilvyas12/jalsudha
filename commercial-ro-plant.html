Which one do I need?

Choose by how much water you use each day. Up to about 2,500 litres a day, a 250 LPH plant is enough. From about 3,000 to 5,000 litres a day, choose 500 LPH. If you are not sure, we test your water's TDS for free in Pratapgarh and recommend the right size and membrane.

What comes with every plant

Sediment and carbon pre-filters, a high-pressure pump, Dove/Duken RO membranes in an FRP pressure vessel and Vectus piping. Options include an automatic PLC panel, auto-flush, level sensors and a water chiller for cold drinking water.

Installation and service

Our team installs the plant at your site and shows your staff how to run it. After installation we offer repairs and Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC), with same-day technician visits in and around Pratapgarh.

Questions

What is the output of a 250 LPH RO plant?

A 250 LPH plant gives about 200 to 500 litres of purified water per hour, which suits sites using around 1,000 to 2,500 litres a day.

Is a 250 or 500 LPH RO plant better for a school?

It depends on daily use. Schools using around 3,000 to 5,000 litres a day are best served by a 500 LPH plant. Smaller schools can use 250 LPH. We confirm this after a free TDS test.

Which membranes do you use?

We use Dove/Duken RO membranes in FRP pressure vessels, with 60 to 65% water recovery.
