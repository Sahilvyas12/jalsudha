Questions

How do I choose between 1000 and 2000 LPH?

Choose by daily water use: a 1000 LPH plant suits around 6,000 to 10,000 litres a day, and a 2000 LPH plant suits 15,000 litres a day or more.

Can the plant run automatically?

Yes. Semi- or fully-automatic PLC control is available, with options such as auto backwash, auto-flush and level sensors.

Do you also supply dosing pumps and chemicals?

Yes. We supply dosing pumps, RO antiscalant chemicals and spare membranes for our plants.
