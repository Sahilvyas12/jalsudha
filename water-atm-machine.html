A complete drinking water point

A Water ATM works best as part of a full setup: an RO plant to purify the water, an industrial chiller to cool it, and the vending machine to dispense it with coins or RFID cards. We can supply and install all of it.

Why RFID cards and keyfobs

Regular users can recharge a card or keyfob instead of carrying coins. We supply the rechargeable RFID cards and keyfobs along with the machine.

Service

Our technicians service the RO plant, chiller and machine, and offer Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) with same-day visits in and around Pratapgarh.

Questions

How does a Water ATM work?

The machine dispenses purified water from an RO plant when the user inserts a coin or taps a rechargeable RFID card or keyfob.

Can the water be chilled?

Yes. We supply industrial water chillers that cool the purified water for Water ATMs.

Do you supply extra RFID cards?

Yes. Rechargeable RFID smart cards and keyfobs are available separately.
